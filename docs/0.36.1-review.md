# RustWTM 0.36.1 release scope and validation

## Scope

A fix release from the visual review of 0.36.0's new pages. IPC is
unchanged at protocol 18 (`crates/wtm-ipc` has no change since 0.36.0), so
a 0.36.0 service and a 0.36.1 window work together.

- Lengths of time are written with the catalog's own hour, minute and
  second units: container alerts and their settings line, a container's
  and a device's recorded history, App history's *Seen* column and the
  watch notices said "1m 15s" and "21h 00m" in every language. A zero part
  is left out ("1 dk", "21 Std."); a day or more is counted in hours, as
  the catalog has no day unit. English keeps its compact form.
- The 1 h, 6 h and 24 h period buttons of App history, recorded incidents
  and a device's recorded history are translated ("24 sa", "24 Std.").
- A value ending in an abbreviation at the end of a sentence ("für
  1 Min.") takes one full stop, not two.
- Turkish: the support bundle's opening sentence is reworded.

## Verification

`cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings`,
`cargo doc` with warnings denied and the 1167 workspace tests (22 ignored)
pass on the developer host; `scripts/ci/verify-release.ps1 -Tag v0.36.1`
passes. A new test checks the durations and period labels in English,
Turkish and German, and the single full stop. CI adds 0.36.0 as an upgrade
baseline, with the SHA-256 of its published MSI (the release asset's digest
and its `.sha256` file agree).

Reviewed visually in English, Turkish and German, light and dark, wide and
compact: Performance (a disk's recorded history), Containers (alerts,
details and the last 24 hours), Events (sources and details), the support
bundle and Settings → This window. Turkish and German were rendered again
after the fix (Performance, Containers, App history and About).

Seen and left as they are: on a compact window the Containers table cuts
German states ("Wird ausg…") and scrolls below the
alerts.
