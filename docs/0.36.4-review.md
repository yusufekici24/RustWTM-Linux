# RustWTM 0.36.4 release scope and validation

## Scope

A fix release for the layout findings the 0.36.2 review left open, the
singular forms not checked then, and numbers in the service's messages.
IPC is unchanged at protocol 18 (`crates/wtm-ipc` has no change since
0.36.0), so a 0.36.x service and a 0.36.4 window work together.

- **Status bar.** It wrapped on a compact window: on Processes the
  inspector menu and the message went to a second line, which took a row
  from the table. The actions now stand at the right on one line and keep
  their size; the facts on the left share what is left, the CPU and memory
  summary shortened first (with the whole text on hover) and left out
  below 80 px. A message stays beside them while 240 px of it fits, or all
  of a shorter one, and otherwise moves to a line of its own.
- **More below.** The button covered the last visible row and its buttons
  even when less than a row was hidden; it now shows only while more than
  48 px is left, and the faded edge alone says the rest.
- **Sideways tables.** A table that scrolls sideways (App history,
  Installed apps, Containers, Ports, Startup) cut its edge column with
  nothing to say it goes on. The side with columns beyond it now fades
  into the panel, beside the rows only. An ellipsis would be wrong there:
  the column is scrolled, not shortened.
- **Numbers in service messages.** A message translated from the service's
  English kept the point of its numbers ("durchschnittlich 12.5% CPU",
  "işlem %40.0"). A value that is a plain decimal number takes the
  language's sign; versions, addresses and file names, which have more
  than one point or other characters, stay as they are.
- **Singular forms.** The five counted phrases gave a count of one the
  plural in Swahili and in Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Telugu,
  Tamil and Kannada (noun plural or plural verb agreement). The singular
  of the well-known tools line says "or", as English does, in those and in
  Bengali, Filipino, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
  Russian and Polish write these as "Label: N", which reads right for one.
- The review fixture's App history finding uses the service's real text,
  so reviews and screenshots show its translation.

## Verification

`cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings`
and the 1174 workspace tests (22 ignored, `--no-fail-fast`) pass on the
developer host;
`scripts/ci/verify-release.ps1 -Tag v0.36.4` passes. New tests:

- the status bar stays on one line on a compact Processes page in every
  language without a message, and with a short one in English, Turkish
  and German; with a long message the actions stay on the version's line
  and the message clears them;
- *More below* shows no button with 30 px hidden and shows it with 200 px;
- which sides of a sideways table fade at its start, middle and end;
- German service messages take the decimal comma, and only plain
  decimals change.

Processes, Disks, App history, Installed apps, Overview, Settings,
Containers, Ports, Startup, Services, Users, Network and Hardware were
rendered in English, Turkish and German, light and dark, wide and compact,
and looked at after the change. CI adds 0.36.3 as an upgrade baseline,
with the SHA-256 of its published MSI (the release asset's digest and its
`.sha256` file agree).

Open: `app::tests::full_app_navigates_every_tab`, which drives the window
on the live system, failed twice in full local runs after the change
(Delete on the selected process did not open the confirmation). It passed
in the six full GUI runs after and in the final workspace run. A row
tooltip is not the cause (egui does not count tooltips as open popups,
which is what makes the list ignore keys); the cause was not found.
